The reset failed with
12 ns to spare.

The controller had 120 ns to reset a qubit. It finished in 108 ns and left the qubit in state 1. Replay the same simulated measurement with a fresh calibration and watch where the two decisions diverge.

Why the flip was skipped

The microwave signal had rotated away from its reference. Using the old reference, the controller reads a 0 at sample 20 and leaves the partner untouched. With the fitted reference, it recognizes a 1 at sample 11 and flips the partner to 0 at 72 ns.

How often does calibration help?

Across 4,000 simulated shots at 35° drift, fitting the reference with 16 labeled pilots raised correct, on-time resets from 2,566 to 3,427. A separate replay tests what happens when the signal shifts again after calibration.
