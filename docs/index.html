THE CALIBRATION PROBLEM

A confident answer can still be wrong.

During readout, state 0 and state 1 trace different paths through two measured voltage channels, I and Q. A phase shift rotates both paths. If the controller keeps comparing against the old paths, it can pick the wrong state early. A few labeled calibration shots give it a way to estimate the rotation.

INSIDE THE SIMULATOR

Rust async in two layers.

NeXosim advances logical time one sample at a time. The circuit actor sends evidence to the readout actor. After accumulating samples, the readout actor sends its decision back with .await. The circuit actor schedules a conditional gate and verifies the target state and deadline.

Tokio coordinates independent benches. JoinSet::spawn_blocking keeps simulation work off the async coordinator, a semaphore bounds concurrency to two benches, and Arc shares immutable inputs.
